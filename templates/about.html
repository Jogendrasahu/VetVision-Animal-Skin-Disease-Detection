About VetVision AI

An advanced veterinary intelligence platform pioneering dual-tier neural vision architectures to make animal health screening fast, transparent, and clinically responsible.

Vision & Purpose

Bridging Veterinary Vision & Clinical Decision Support

Dermatological and ocular lesions in domestic animals often present with overlapping clinical signs. VetVision AI provides deep-feature differential screening to support earlier veterinary consultation and reduce preventable suffering.

🎯

Clinical Decision Support

Rather than offering a black-box, single-label diagnosis, VetVision AI synthesizes a full differential spectrum—providing Top-3 ranked probability distributions, consensus agreement, and structured clinical advice.

🛡️

Dual-Tier Safety Architecture

Traditional vision models fail silently when fed humans, furniture, or incorrect species. VetVision AI deploys an upfront MobileNetV2 Gatekeeper to enforce biological domain boundaries before disease models are ever triggered.

Responsible AI

AI Safety, Ethics & Scientific Transparency

Guiding principles ensuring human-in-the-loop animal care.

Non-Diagnostic Positioning

VetVision AI never claims to replace physical examination, fungal culture, wood's lamp evaluation, deep skin scrape cytology, or histopathology performed by certified veterinary clinicians.

Zero Fabricated Medical Data

All clinical descriptions, symptoms, and precautions are sourced strictly from established veterinary textbooks and consensus statements. No generative hallucinations or invented metrics are allowed.

Data Privacy & Confidentiality

Uploaded animal imagery is processed securely and stored exclusively in your local SQLite database. Patient history can be purged or exported at any time via the History dashboard.
