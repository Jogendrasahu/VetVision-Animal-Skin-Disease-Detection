3. Neural Architecture & Inference Pipeline

Six-stage end-to-end clinical inference pipeline connecting pre-ingestion image quality checks, MobileNetV2 Gatekeeper out-of-distribution rejection, and species-specific EfficientNetB0 classifiers.

1
Quality & Ingestion QC
Resolution verification (≥64×64 to 224 × 224 standard input), Laplacian variance blur detection, luminance & dark frame filtering.
2
MobileNetV2 Gatekeeper
1,000-synset ImageNet filter. Rejects humans, vehicles, random objects, and non-animal artifacts.
3
Species Isolation
Validates Canine, Feline, or Bovine semantic boundaries. Blocks cross-species diagnostic contamination.
4
EfficientNetB0 Classifier
RAW 0–255 [0, 255] float RGB tensor fed directly to species classifier with internal Keras Rescaling layers.
5
Softmax & Differential
Computes Top-3 ranked differential predictions and uncalibrated activation confidence gauge.
6
11-Field Clinical Guidance
Maps predictions to validated symptoms, contagion levels, home care, urgent triage & CSV export.

Scientific Transparency: Multi-Class ROC & Precision-Recall Curves Rationale

Continuous Receiver Operating Characteristic (ROC) and Precision-Recall (PR) curves require full un-thresholded soft probability tensors across all non-ground-truth classes for every validation instance. Because original Phase 4 batch evaluation routines recorded Top-1 classification reports and discrete confusion matrices rather than 718×34 continuous floating-point probability tensors, continuous ROC and PR curves are omitted here to uphold strict scientific reproducibility and avoid synthetic spline approximations.

10. Clinical Limitations & Ethical Disclosures

Core academic principles governing the appropriate veterinary interpretation, technical boundaries, and safety protocols of VetVision AI.

1. Clinical Decision Support Scope
VetVision AI is an educational and preliminary triage decision-support platform. It does not provide definitive medical diagnoses, dispense pharmaceutical prescriptions, or replace formal veterinary dermatological examination, cytology, bacterial culture, skin scraping, or biopsy.
2. Preliminary Local Test Cohorts
All reported accuracy, precision, and recall metrics are evaluated on curated local test partitions (718 total samples). Because historical test partitions were not derived from an external prospective multi-center clinical cohort, performance in field conditions with atypical lighting or novel breeds may vary.
3. Softmax Heuristic Calibration
Confidence gauge readings and class percentage scores represent raw softmax activation values from convolutional feature maps. They serve as ranking heuristics for differential exploration and do not reflect calibrated epidemiological prevalence or true Bayesian posterior disease likelihoods.
4. Strict Species Domain Boundaries
The platform is strictly bounded to Canine (Dog), Feline (Cat), and Bovine (Cow) species. Caprine (Goat) dataset excluded; Equine (Horse), Avian (Bird), and exotic species are strictly unsupported. Out-of-distribution animals must be rejected at the Gatekeeper stage to prevent invalid diagnoses.
5. Experimental Video Frame Aggregation
Video diagnosis extracts equidistant frames and aggregates predictions via majority consensus voting. Rapid motion, camera vibration, out-of-focus frames, or shifting illumination can introduce frame-level variances. Video outputs are strictly experimental.
6. Scientific Transparency & No Fabrication
VetVision AI enforces strict adherence to scientific reproducibility. No synthetic training curves or fabricated accuracy claims are presented. Unarchived training history logs are explicitly declared as unavailable rather than synthetically simulated.
