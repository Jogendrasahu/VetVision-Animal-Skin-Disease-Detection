Pipeline Architecture

How VetVision AI Works

A transparent, dual-tier inference pipeline designed to prevent false positives and deliver actionable veterinary intelligence.

1

Multimodal Input

Upload a single high-res photo, batch up to 5 multi-angle perspectives, an MP4/WebM video stream, or snap directly with your webcam.

2

Tier-1 Gatekeeper

MobileNetV2 neural filter validates the image for blur, lighting, and verifies species alignment to prevent invalid out-of-distribution queries.

3

Deep Vision Analysis

Species-specialized EfficientNetB0 models extract deep lesion patterns, outputting calibrated softmax confidence probabilities and Top-3 differentials.

4

Clinical Guidance

11-field structured report delivers symptoms, etiology, contagiousness, home care, red-flag emergency symptoms, and Google Maps vet locator.
